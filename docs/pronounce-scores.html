One clip, four scores

You said “dog”, the pill says 94, the big number says 96%. Neither is wrong — they are two different scores Azure computes in the same response.

The short answer: Azure grades every clip at four levels at once — phoneme, syllable, word, full text. Each level is aggregated from the phonemes and then re-refined with its own weighting, so adjacent levels land on slightly different numbers. The pill shows the syllable’s score (94); the big percentage shows the full-text score (96).
